Nine sensor channels.

Acceleration, rotation and the magnetic field, each along three axes: nine channels describing the phone’s movement.

02 / GROUP

Five-second windows.

At 100 Hz, a five-second window holds 500 samples per channel. The same physical interval goes to each model.

03 / COMBINE

Weighted probability fusion.

Classifiers using frozen NuTime, MantisV2 and TiRex representations contribute probabilities with weights 0.54, 0.29 and 0.17.

What “frozen” means
The pretrained representation models remain fixed. The study compares the representations and downstream classifier/fusion choices.

Study scope: five frozen models were compared. The weights shown here belong to the selected final fusion; the animation illustrates the method with a sample Walking output.
