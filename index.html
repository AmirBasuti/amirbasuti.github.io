01 / MACHINE LEARNING · SHL 2026

SHL: frozen-model
probability fusion.

We compared five frozen models and early/late fusion strategies. The selected system weights NuTime, MantisV2 and TiRex predictions at 0.54, 0.29 and 0.17. Our team placed first in SHL 2026.

02 / MOVEMENT & HEALTH

FoG detection
from four IMUs.

Our FoG-STAR study uses accelerometer and gyroscope recordings from four IMUs, frozen time-series representations, and a window-level classifier. The evaluation is offline; it does not establish real-time warning performance.
